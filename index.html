About the entity

One legal structure. Many ventures.

CVPE is a Finland-registered holding and innovation hatchery. Rather than operating a single business, we identify, structure, and manage a portfolio of independent ventures under one legal entity — providing the governance, capital allocation, and operational discipline each project needs to grow, without requiring every new idea to incorporate on its own from day one.

Where a conventional holding company sits above already-finished businesses, CVPE gets involved earlier: testing concepts under controlled conditions, and either spinning successful ones out as independent companies or continuing to hold and steward them long-term. The entity itself is domiciled in Lohja, Finland, and operates under Finnish corporate law, with registration that any partner, supplier, or institution can independently verify through the Finnish Patent and Registration Office's business information system.

How we operate

Governance first

Every venture under CVPE operates against a shared standard of financial reporting, contractual discipline, and legal accountability — set at the holding level and enforced consistently underneath it.

Capital with intent

Resources are allocated deliberately across the portfolio, based on the stage and risk profile of each venture, rather than distributed evenly or reactively.

Built to last, or built to leave

Some ventures are designed to be spun out as independent companies once they're ready. Others remain under CVPE for the long term. Either path is planned for from the start, not decided under pressure.

Technology support

Also built with our support, held elsewhere.
